# ORAC Creative Brief

This is the full creative context for the ORAC Holding website redesign. It is a reference document, not a task list — read the relevant sections before substantial design or homepage work. Confirmed facts derived from this brief live in [ORAC-DECISIONS.md](ORAC-DECISIONS.md); the staged plan for acting on it lives in [ORAC-REDESIGN-SPEC.md](ORAC-REDESIGN-SPEC.md).

## Brand objective

ORAC Holding must feel like a sophisticated parent business ecosystem containing ventures across:

- International trade
- Events
- Photography and cinematography
- Fashion
- Textile solutions
- Future ventures

The website should feel: premium, original, cinematic, editorial, modern, refined, confident, intentional, memorable, technically polished.

It must **not** feel like: a generic corporate template, a normal startup landing page, an AI-generated website, a collection of disconnected animations, a direct copy of another website, or a conventional service-card layout.

## Existing website strengths to preserve

The existing ORAC website already provides:

- Business clarity
- Premium credibility
- Sophisticated typography
- A useful business hierarchy
- Commercial usefulness
- A refined foundation

**Do not destroy these strengths.** They are confirmed present in the live implementation — see [ORAC-CURRENT-AUDIT.md](ORAC-CURRENT-AUDIT.md) for the evidence.

## Existing website weaknesses to improve

The current website still feels too close to a polished corporate website. Likely weaknesses to evaluate (confirmed or not against the actual rendered site in the audit) include:

- A conventional opening
- Predictable section sequencing
- Ventures represented too much like cards
- Limited narrative progression
- Excessive dependence on text
- Leadership appearing too early
- Luxury communicated too heavily through gold
- Weak transitions between ventures
- No single ownable creative idea
- A lack of memorable progression and discovery

Every one of these has been checked against the actual rendered website rather than assumed — see the audit for which are confirmed and with what evidence.

## Creative benchmark

The approved creative benchmark is **Izanami Studio** (izanami.studio).

Learn from its: strong central concept, cinematic opening, editorial layouts, unexpected but controlled compositions, negative space, layered imagery, immersive progression, chapter-based storytelling, refined typography, purposeful motion, cohesive art direction, authored feeling.

**Do not copy** its: layout, colour palette, typography, graphics, animations, cursor, navigation, text, sequence, or visual identity.

The objective is not to make ORAC look like Izanami. The objective is to make ORAC equally intentional, original, and authored through its own concept.

> **Note on prior references:** earlier project notes (a prior `CLAUDE.md` found in this repository, superseded by this documentation set) also named Apple as a reference for refinement/restraint/interaction quality, and "Bakinatajna" as a reference for layered product presentation and spatial motion. These are not part of the confirmed brief above and are not repeated as instructions here. Whether they remain active references is recorded as a pending decision — see [ORAC-DECISIONS.md](ORAC-DECISIONS.md).

## Exact transformation objective

Combine:

**From the current ORAC website** — clarity, credibility, correct business hierarchy, real content, premium positioning, commercial usefulness.

**From creative references** — original composition, cinematic progression, immersive transitions, layered visual depth, strong art direction, chapter-based storytelling, memorable interaction, a coherent atmosphere.

The result must feel specifically created for ORAC.

## Central creative concept

Working direction: **One origin. Multiple worlds.**

ORAC Holding is the origin. Each venture becomes its own world:

### ORAC International

Its visual world should communicate: global movement, trade, routes, materials, products, precision, reliability, origin and destination.

Avoid: cliché rotating globes, generic shipping animations, random world maps, supermarket-like presentation.

### ORAC Eventus × VELORAWED

Its visual world should communicate: memory, emotion, human moments, planning, storytelling, cinematic documentation, reliable execution.

Avoid: generic wedding templates, ordinary photography galleries, random image collages, overly decorative romantic styling.

### ORAC Luxe × The House of Azrin

Its visual world should communicate: fabric, texture, material, form, silhouette, craft, precision, modern luxury.

Relevant confirmed information:

- The House of Azrin is the flagship fashion brand of ORAC Luxe
- Founder and Creative Director: Ashrin Rahmathullah
- Premium fashion, textile solutions, private-label work, handcrafted artistry, modern luxury, precision, timeless design

Avoid: generic fashion e-commerce styling, invented collections, invented capabilities, fake business history.

### ORAC Evolution

Its visual world should communicate: emergence, possibility, incompleteness, controlled mystery, a future taking shape.

It is still under development. Do not invent its services. Avoid generic futuristic claims and excessive science-fiction visuals.

**Status note:** ORAC Evolution does not currently exist in the codebase — no route, no data entry, no navigation item, no content. See [ORAC-CURRENT-AUDIT.md](ORAC-CURRENT-AUDIT.md) and [ORAC-DECISIONS.md](ORAC-DECISIONS.md).

## Homepage progression

The homepage should broadly progress through:

1. Origin
2. Expansion
3. Exploration of the ventures
4. Connection between the worlds
5. Credibility
6. Leadership
7. Continuation or contact

The page must not feel like independent sections stacked vertically.

## Opening direction

Do not retain a generic heading, paragraph, and button hero as the entire opening. The opening should establish: ORAC as the parent identity, four connected ventures, a feeling of entering a larger ecosystem, scale, anticipation, a memorable visual signature.

Possible mechanisms:

- One form separating into four paths
- A shared frame transforming between ventures
- Venture worlds emerging from one visual system
- A pathway or visual object evolving across the page

Choose **one** coherent mechanism. Do not combine unrelated concepts.

## Ventures as chapters

The ventures should not primarily appear as a standard four-card grid. Each should feel like a chapter with its own composition, image treatment, atmosphere, motion behaviour, typography relationship, and transition into the next chapter.

They must remain connected through: shared navigation, shared spacing, shared interaction principles, ORAC typography, one transition language, the parent visual system.

## Visual design rules

Premium quality must come from typography, spacing, proportion, image direction, motion, material treatment, contrast, precision, restraint, interaction quality — **not primarily from gold**. Gold may remain a controlled accent.

Use editorial composition where appropriate: asymmetry, controlled overlap, large negative space, layered text and imagery, full-bleed imagery, precise cropping, vertical labels, selective grid-breaking elements, text placed in relation to image subjects.

All compositions must remain responsive and readable.

## Motion rules

Motion should establish hierarchy, guide attention, connect chapters, reveal information, strengthen storytelling, and communicate movement between ventures. It must be smooth, controlled, cinematic, consistent, performant, purposeful.

Avoid: repeated fade-up animation everywhere, constant floating text, aggressive zooming, excessive scroll hijacking, purposeless parallax, heavy cursor effects, mobile performance problems.

## Responsive rules

Design and verify for at least: 1440px desktop, 1024px tablet, 768px boundary, 390px mobile.

Mobile must not be a compressed desktop design. Recompose layouts and simplify motion when necessary.

## Content rules

Writing must be clear, concise, premium, human, specific, credible. Avoid generic AI phrases and unsupported claims.
